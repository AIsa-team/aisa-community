# AIsa Community Competitions

Once or twice a month we pick winners from community submissions. Winners get **AIsa credits** and **exposure on official AIsa media channels** — exact prizes are defined per cycle.

## How a cycle works

1. **Announce** — a new `competitions/YYYY-MM/` folder lands with the theme, deadline and prizes, plus a pinned Discussion.
2. **Enter** — submit (or substantially update) a project with `competition: "YYYY-MM"` in its metadata before the deadline. CI labels entries automatically.
3. **Judge** — after the deadline, an anonymous panel scores every qualifying entry against the rubric below, independently; the average decides. Judging is **panel-only**.
4. **Announce winners** — a ranked podium of up to three places (🥇 🥈 🥉). `winners.yaml` lands in the cycle folder, the Hall of Fame in the README updates automatically, and winners are featured on official channels.

## Judging rubric

| Criterion | Weight | What we look for |
|---|---|---|
| Creativity | 10% | Original idea, unexpected use of AIsa |
| Usefulness | 40% | Solves a real problem; would people actually use it |
| AIsa integration depth | 35% | How central and well-used the AIsa endpoints are |
| Craft | 15% | Code quality, docs, demo quality |

## Terms

- **Eligibility:** anyone with a GitHub account except AIsa-team maintainers and competition judges. One account per person.
- **Entries:** up to 2 per person per cycle. Entries must be your own work (collaborations allowed — credit everyone; the prize goes to the submitting account).
- **Originality:** entries must be new submissions, or existing ones substantially updated during the cycle window.
- **Fair play:** sockpuppets, plagiarism, or attempts to influence judges disqualify the entry and may lead to a ban (see CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
- **Judging:** the panel is anonymous and its decisions are final. Places may go unawarded if a cycle lacks qualifying entries; honorable mentions may be added in the announcement.
- **Prizes:** AIsa credits are delivered privately — winners will be contacted through GitHub to arrange delivery. **Never post your email or account details in this repo.** Credits are non-transferable and have no cash value.
- **Feature rights:** by entering, you allow AIsa to feature your entry (name, description, screenshots, links, your GitHub handle) on official AIsa channels, with credit.
- **Licensing:** your work remains yours, under the license you declared.
