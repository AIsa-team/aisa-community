# AIsa Community Competitions

Once or twice a month we pick winners from community submissions. Winners get **AIsa credits** and a **feature on official AIsa media channels**.

## How a cycle works

1. **Announce** — a new `competitions/YYYY-MM/` folder lands with the theme, deadline and prizes, plus a pinned Discussion.
2. **Enter** — submit (or substantially update) a project or skill with `competition: "YYYY-MM"` in its metadata before the deadline. CI labels entries automatically.
3. **Judge** — after the deadline, entries are scored against the rubric below. A community-vote Discussion may contribute up to 25% of the score.
4. **Announce winners** — up to two per cycle, **Best Project** and **Best Skill**. `winners.yaml` lands in the cycle folder, the Hall of Fame in the README updates automatically, and winners are featured on official channels.

## Judging rubric

| Criterion | Weight | What we look for |
|---|---|---|
| Creativity | 10% | Original idea, unexpected use of AIsa |
| Usefulness | 40% | Solves a real problem; would people actually use it |
| AIsa integration depth | 35% | How central and well-used the AIsa skills are |
| Craft | 15% | Code quality, docs, demo quality |

## Terms

- **Eligibility:** anyone with a GitHub account except AIsa-team maintainers and competition judges. One account per person.
- **Entries:** up to 2 per person per cycle, across both tracks. Entries must be your own work (collaborations allowed — credit everyone; the prize goes to the submitting account).
- **Originality:** entries must be new submissions, or existing ones substantially updated during the cycle window.
- **Fair play:** vote brigading, sockpuppets, or plagiarism disqualify the entry and may lead to a ban (see CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
- **Judging:** judges' decisions are final. Each cycle awards at most one Best Project and one Best Skill; a category may go unawarded if it lacks qualifying entries, and honorable mentions may be added in the announcement.
- **Prizes:** AIsa credits are delivered privately — winners will be contacted through GitHub to arrange delivery. **Never post your email or account details in this repo.** Credits are non-transferable and have no cash value.
- **Feature rights:** by entering, you allow AIsa to feature your entry (name, description, screenshots, links, your GitHub handle) on official AIsa channels, with credit.
- **Licensing:** your work remains yours, under the license you declared.
