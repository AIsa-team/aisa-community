# Code of Conduct

This community exists so people can build, share, and celebrate work made with AIsa. Keep it a place worth showing up to.

## Expectations

- **Be respectful.** Critique work, not people. No harassment, hate speech, personal attacks, or unwelcome contact — in issues, PRs, Discussions, or competition threads.
- **Be honest.** Submit your own work (or clearly credited collaborations). No plagiarized projects, no fake demos, no astroturfed votes.
- **Be constructive.** Review feedback should help the submitter improve. "This is bad" is not a review.
- **Play competitions fair.** No vote brigading, sockpuppet accounts, or sabotaging other entries. Judges' decisions are final.
- **Respect maintainer time.** Read CONTRIBUTING.md before submitting; respond to review feedback or the PR may be closed as stale after 30 days.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, close, or remove contributions and comments that violate this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for repeated or egregious violations. Competition entries from banned accounts are disqualified.

Report violations privately to the maintainers (see SECURITY.md for contact channels). Reports are handled confidentially.

*Adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).*
